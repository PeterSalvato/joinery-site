Lesson 1.6: Week 1 Deliverable

Independent practice. No template this time.

Lesson 1.5 was your first full decomposition cycle. This is the second — different project, less scaffolding.

Pick a second workflow from your own work. Different project than Lesson 1.5. The full cycle, no scaffolding.

  1. Identify a compound prompt or workflow.
  2. Decompose it into independent specialist prompts.
  3. Run both versions: the original compound prompt and the decomposed set.
  4. Synthesize the decomposed outputs into a unified brief. Resolve the conflicts. Find any convergences neither specialist named. Set the cross-system rules.
  5. Compare the compound output to your synthesized brief.

Submit

  • The compound prompt and its output
  • Your decomposed prompts and their individual outputs
  • Your synthesized brief
  • A short reflection — 3 to 5 sentences

The reflection question

Where in the decomposed run did the output go somewhere your compound prompt wouldn't have allowed?
