Module 1: Task Decomposition

6 lessons · About 90 minutes of reading

Practitioners who use AI lose authorship. This module teaches you to use the attunement skill you already have to stay in the author's seat.

You already attune to the receiving systems for your work: the audience, the reader, the client, the subject. This module applies that same attunement skill to AI as a new receiving system. You'll see why compound prompts degrade, watch what changes when you decompose the same brief, and do it yourself on your own work.

What best describes your primary practice?

You can switch at any time inside the lessons.

Lesson 1.1

The Frame Break

Why the instinct to add more detail to a prompt is the wrong move. The pattern you already use when reading a room, applied to a different system.
Lesson 1.2

The Compound Instruction Problem

A real brief, run through the compound approach. Watch what the AI actually does when you give it five jobs at once.
Lesson 1.3

The Decomposition

Same brief, decomposed into specialist tasks. One job per prompt. The output changes in ways that matter.
Lesson 1.4

The Synthesis Step

The part AI cannot do. You step through the specialist outputs, make tracked decisions, and compile the creative direction brief.
Lesson 1.5

Decompose Your Own Work

Apply the full cycle to your own project. Decompose, run specialists, synthesize, articulate what your judgment added.
Lesson 1.6

Week 1 Deliverable

Your decomposition brief, synthesis decisions, and reflection on what changed when you decomposed the work.
